# Returns and Refunds Policy

Rules for accepting returns and issuing refunds in NovaMart stores. Applies to purchases made in store and on novamart.com.

## Standard return window

Most products can be returned within 30 days of the purchase date, in original condition and with original packaging.

Electronics (TVs, laptops, tablets, phones, headphones, cameras and gaming consoles) have a shorter window: 15 days from the purchase date.

The purchase date is the date printed on the receipt. For online orders, it is the delivery date shown in the order record.

## Holiday extension

Purchases made between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 15 of the following year. The holiday extension applies to all categories, including electronics.

## Proof of purchase

A receipt, an order number or the card used for payment is accepted as proof of purchase. Look up online orders by order number.

Without proof of purchase, the item can only be returned for store credit at the lowest price the item sold for in the last 90 days, and only with a valid photo ID. Record the ID number in the return form.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method:

- Credit or debit card: 5 to 10 business days to appear on the statement.
- Cash: refunded in cash up to 200 dollars. Above that, a check is mailed within 10 business days.
- Gift card or store credit: refunded as store credit.

Refunds above 500 dollars require a manager's approval before processing.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the price tag or receipt.
- Opened software, video games, music and movies.
- Personal care items that have been opened (razors, toothbrushes, cosmetics).
- Underwear, swimwear and earrings, for hygiene reasons.
- Custom or personalized products.
- Gift cards.

Items that are damaged or defective follow the Damaged and Defective Items policy, even if they are in a final sale category.
