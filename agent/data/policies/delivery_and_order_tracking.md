# Delivery and Order Tracking

How to look up an order, what each status means, and what to tell the customer.

## Looking up an order

Use the order lookup tool with the order number (format NM-#### printed on the confirmation email). Never guess an order's status from memory. The lookup returns the current status, the carrier, the estimated delivery date and the last tracking event.

## Delivery times

Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days after the order ships. Express shipping: 1 to 2 business days. Store pickup orders are ready within 2 hours during store opening times, and held for 7 days.

## Order statuses and what to tell the customer

- Processing: the order was received and is being prepared. Tell the customer it will ship within 1 business day.
- Shipped or In transit: the carrier has the package. Share the carrier and tracking number.
- Delayed: the carrier reported a delay. Apologize, share the new estimated date, and offer a 10 dollar NovaPoints credit (1,000 points) for the inconvenience. No approval is needed for this credit.
- Delivered: the carrier confirmed delivery. If the customer says they did not receive it, follow the missing package procedure.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled. Refunds for cancelled orders are issued automatically within 5 business days.
- Return in progress: the customer sent the item back. Refund is issued after the returns center inspects the item, usually 3 to 5 business days after arrival.

## Missing package procedure

If the status is Delivered but the customer did not receive the package:

1. Ask the customer to check with neighbors, building reception and the carrier's delivery photo if available.
2. If the package is still missing 48 hours after the delivery timestamp, file a Missing Package Claim in the POS. The claim triggers a replacement or refund within 3 business days.
3. Claims above 300 dollars are reviewed by the Operations team before approval.

## Changing or cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled by the customer or by a store employee while the status is Processing. Once the status is Shipped, the order cannot be cancelled; the customer can refuse delivery or return the item after it arrives.
