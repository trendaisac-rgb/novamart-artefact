# Gift Cards and Store Credit

Rules for selling, redeeming and replacing gift cards and store credit.

## Gift cards

- Gift cards are sold in amounts from 10 to 500 dollars. They never expire.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, except where state law requires cash back for small remaining balances (under 10 dollars in most states; check the state list in the POS).
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded once activated.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

A gift card can only be replaced if the customer has the original purchase receipt or the card number. Replace the remaining balance on a new card. Without receipt or card number, the card cannot be replaced.

## Store credit

Store credit is issued as a NovaMart Credit Card (a physical or digital card) for no-receipt returns and some refunds.

- Store credit expires 12 months after it is issued.
- Store credit can be used in any NovaMart store or on novamart.com.
- Store credit cannot be converted to cash.
- Store credit balance can be checked at any register with the card number.

## Partial payments

A purchase can combine a gift card or store credit with any other payment method. Apply the gift card or store credit first, then the remaining balance.
