# Exchanges Policy

How to handle exchanges for size, color, model or a different product.

## Exchange window

Exchanges follow the same window as returns: 30 days for most products and 15 days for electronics. The holiday extension also applies.

## Same product, different size or color

Exchange at no cost if the new item has the same price. No manager approval is needed.

If the new size or color is out of stock in the store, offer one of these options:

- Check stock in nearby stores and reserve the item for pickup.
- Place a ship-to-home order at the same price, free shipping.
- Process a return and refund.

## Different product

Process the exchange as a return followed by a new sale. The customer pays the difference if the new item costs more, or receives the difference back to the original payment method if it costs less.

## Items that cannot be exchanged

Final sale items cannot be exchanged, not even for a different size or color. These are the same final sale items as in the Returns and Refunds Policy:

- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the price tag or receipt.
- Opened software, video games, music and movies.
- Personal care items that have been opened (razors, toothbrushes, cosmetics).
- Underwear, swimwear and earrings, for hygiene reasons.
- Custom or personalized products.
- Gift cards.

Items that are damaged or defective follow the Damaged and Defective Items policy, even if they are in a final sale category.

## Exchange without receipt

Same-product exchanges for size or color can be done without a receipt if the item is clearly a NovaMart product with an intact tag. Any other exchange without proof of purchase follows the no-receipt rule in the Returns and Refunds Policy (store credit at the lowest price in the last 90 days).
