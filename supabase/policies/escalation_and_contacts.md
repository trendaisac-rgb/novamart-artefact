# Escalation and Contacts

When a store employee must escalate, and who to contact.

## Operations helpdesk

The Operations helpdesk handles questions that are not covered by the policies, system errors in the POS, and approvals above the store manager's limits.

- Hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 (store local time).
- Channel: ticket through this assistant or the Ops Portal. Phone (extension 4000) only for issues blocking a sale in progress.
- Target response time: 2 hours for blocking issues, 1 business day for everything else.

## When to escalate to the store manager

- Refunds above 500 dollars.
- Price match differences above 100 dollars.
- Any suspected fraud or misuse.
- Customer complaints about employee behavior.
- Any situation where the customer asks to speak to a manager.

## When to escalate to the Operations team

- Missing package claims above 300 dollars.
- Questions not covered by any policy document.
- Requests for exceptions to a policy (for example, a return outside the window).
- POS errors that prevent a return, refund or price match from being processed.

## What to include in an escalation

Store number, order number (if any), a one-line summary of the customer's request, what policy was checked, and what the customer was told so far. Escalations without this information are sent back to the store.

## Human Resources, payroll and scheduling

Questions about pay, benefits, shifts and time off are not handled by Operations. Employees should use the HR Portal or call HR at extension 2000.
