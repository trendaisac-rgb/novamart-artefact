# NovaPoints Loyalty Program

How customers earn and use NovaPoints, and how to handle common questions at the register.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point per dollar spent, before tax, on all purchases except gift cards. 500 points are worth 5 dollars.

Points appear in the account within 24 hours of the purchase. Online orders earn points when the order ships.

## Enrolling a customer

Enrollment is free and takes one minute at any register or on novamart.com. The customer needs a phone number and an email address. Points from the current purchase can be added if the customer enrolls before paying.

## Using points

Points can be redeemed at checkout in multiples of 500 (5 dollars). Points cannot be redeemed for cash, gift cards or shipping fees.

## Points and returns

When an item is returned, the points earned on that item are deducted from the account. If the account does not have enough points, the deduction is applied to the refund amount.

## Expiration

Points expire after 18 months without any purchase on the account. A purchase of any amount resets the counter.

## Missing points

If points are missing from a purchase, the customer can present the receipt at any register within 60 days and the cashier can add the points manually. After 60 days, the request goes to the Operations helpdesk.
