# Damaged and Defective Items Policy

What to do when a customer reports an item that arrived damaged or stopped working.

## Within 30 days of purchase

Offer a replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice. This applies to all categories, including electronics and final sale items.

Ask the customer to bring the item and all accessories. Fill out a Damaged Item Report in the POS before processing the replacement or refund.

## After 30 days

Defects after 30 days are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, not by NovaMart. Give the customer the manufacturer's support contact printed on the product manual or packaging.

Exception: products sold with a NovaMart Protection Plan are handled by the Protection Plan desk (extension 4400) at any time during the plan.

## Large appliances and furniture delivered to the home

Visible delivery damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Ask the customer to send photos to the store email. After 48 hours, the case is treated as a standard defect claim.

## Photos and documentation

Always attach at least one photo of the damage to the Damaged Item Report. Reports without photos are rejected by the returns center and the store absorbs the cost.

## Suspected misuse or fraud

If the damage looks like misuse (water damage, cracked screen with no packaging damage, missing serial number), do not refuse the customer directly. Escalate to the store manager and to the Operations helpdesk with the report number.
