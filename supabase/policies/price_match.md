# Price Match Policy

When and how NovaMart matches a competitor's price.

## Who we match

We match the current advertised price of these retailers: Target, Walmart, Best Buy, Costco (members' price), and Amazon only for items sold and shipped by Amazon itself.

We do not match third-party marketplace sellers (including Amazon Marketplace, eBay and Walmart Marketplace), auction sites, membership-only flash sales, or prices that require a coupon code.

## Conditions

- The item must be identical: same brand, model number, color and size.
- The competitor must have the item in stock at the time of the request.
- The competitor's price must be shown on their official website or printed ad. A screenshot from a search engine is not enough; open the product page.
- The price match is per item, limited to one unit per customer per day.

## Timing

Price matches can be requested at the time of purchase or up to 14 days after the purchase. After the purchase, the customer must present the receipt and the difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## Exclusions

No price match on clearance, open-box, refurbished items, Black Friday week (the Monday before Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday), doorbuster deals, bundle offers, or items sold by NovaMart Marketplace sellers.

## Approval

Price matches up to 100 dollars in difference can be approved by any cashier. Above 100 dollars, a shift lead or manager must approve.
